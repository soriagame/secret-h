About This App

Unofficial fan adaptation

This is a personal, non-commercial companion app built by a fan for playing Secret Hitler with friends. It is not an official product. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or connected to Goat, Wolf & Cabbage or any of the game's creators or publishers.

The original game

Secret Hitler was designed by Mike Boxleiter, Tommy Maranges and Max Temkin, with illustration by Mackenzie Schubert, and published by Goat, Wolf & Cabbage. All credit for the game's design, rules and identity belongs to them. If you enjoy it, buy a physical copy and support the people who made it.

Changes made to the original

This is a modified adaptation, not a reproduction. The original is a physical tabletop game with printed cards, boards and envelopes. Changes made here include:

  • Reimplemented as a digital multi-device web app rather than physical components.
  • Secret roles dealt automatically to each player's own phone, replacing envelopes and the closed-eyes night phase.
  • Voting conducted privately on each device, replacing physical Ja/Nein ballot cards.
  • The policy deck, election tracker and presidential powers tracked automatically.
  • Entirely original visual design, typography, colour palette and wording — none of the original artwork or graphic design is used.
  • Added features with no printed equivalent: a proceedings log, animal avatars for players in the lobby, and full-screen announcements of policies and election results.

What is and isn't reproduced here

This app implements the game's rules and provides secret role dealing and anonymous voting. It contains none of the original artwork, graphic design, box art or printed components — all visual design here is original. The name is used only to describe which game this accompanies.

Historical note

Secret Hitler is a social deduction game about the danger of creeping authoritarianism and how ordinary institutions can be captured from within. Its subject is treated as a cautionary one. Nothing here endorses fascism or the historical figure the game is named for.
